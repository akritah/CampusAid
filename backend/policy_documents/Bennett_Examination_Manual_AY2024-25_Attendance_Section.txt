BENNETT UNIVERSITY — EXAMINATION MANUAL 2024–25
Section 10: Attendance Requirements
Official source: https://www.bennett.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Examination-Manual-2024-25-.pdf

Undergraduate and integrated-program students must have a minimum attendance of 75% in each course during a semester to be eligible to appear for end-semester examinations. Lectures, tutorials, and practical sessions are counted together as applicable. A student with less than 75% attendance in a course is awarded a DB grade in that course, regardless of performance in semester tests. Attendance is counted for the whole semester, and scheduled classes count as one unit regardless of contact hours.

The manual says the requirement applies irrespective of medical or other leave. It permits relaxation of up to 10% on specified exceptional grounds, including continuous illness of two weeks supported by a medical certificate, approved university representation, or a special personal reason recommended by the Dean and approved by the Vice Chancellor.
