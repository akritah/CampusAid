# Policy Q&A documents

Place official `.txt`, `.md`, `.pdf`, or `.docx` files here, or upload them through the authenticated `POST /policy/documents` endpoint as an admin or warden. Retrieved text is provided to Gemini for a concise answer with filename citations; the interface also displays the matched passages. When no relevant passage is found, Gemini provides general guidance without claiming it is university policy.

`Bennett_Examination_Manual_AY2024-25_Attendance_Section.txt` is a searchable transcription of Section 10 from the official manual PDF. Several other PDFs are scans and may require OCR before their text can be retrieved.
