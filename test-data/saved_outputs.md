## Attendance eligibility

**Input:** What is the attendance criteria to appear in exams at Bennett?

**Output:** To be eligible to appear for end-semester examinations, undergraduate and integrated-program students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each course during a semester, which applies irrespective of medical or other leave. Lectures, tutorials, and practical sessions are counted together, and a relaxation of up to 10% is permitted on specified exceptional grounds (such as continuous illness of two weeks supported by medical documentation). Students with less than 75% attendance are awarded a DB grade in that course.

**Source:** `Bennett_Examination_Manual_AY2024-25_Attendance_Section.txt` (transcribed from Section 10 of the [official Bennett Examination Manual](https://www.bennett.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Examination-Manual-2024-25-.pdf)).
